## 🎨 Image Specifications (Recommended)

- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Category Images:** Square format (1:1 aspect ratio), recommended 800x800px
- **Product Images:** Square format (1:1 aspect ratio), recommended 800x800px
- **User Avatar:** Square format (1:1 aspect ratio), recommended 400x400px
- **File Size:** Optimize images (keep under 500KB each for web performance)
- **Background:** Clean, white or light backgrounds work best for product images
